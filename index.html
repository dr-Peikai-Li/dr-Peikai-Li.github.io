A little about me

I enjoy novels and philosophy, and taking photographs—especially portraits and landscapes. During my undergraduate years, when I had more time to explore different interests, I tried writing novels, breaking, and playing the guitar—though I’m still very much a beginner on the guitar.

Over time, research became the interest I kept returning to. These days, I still find time for the occasional novel, but much of my time outside work belongs to my family. My son is becoming quite a little rascal and usually has a better plan for my afternoon: a story together or a trip to the playground.
